# Special Care 360 — Guide for Teachers and Therapists

## 1. Account and login
1. **Register** with your `@specialcare360.com` email, choosing **Teacher** or **Therapist** — or the
   admin creates your account directly.
2. Wait for admin approval, then log in with your role selected.
3. You see **only the students an admin assigned to you**. If a child is missing, ask an admin.

Forgot password: type your email on the login page and select **Forgot password**.
On a phone, install the app: Chrome → **Install app**, or Safari → **Share → Add to Home Screen**.

## 3. Teacher tasks

### Submit the daily care journal
**Daily Care** → student → today's date. Record each meal (or **not observed**), add mood entries
through the day, hygiene and notes → **Submit**. The parent and any linked caregivers are notified.
Submitting again for the same day replaces that day's journal.

### Record medication
**Students** → student → **Medical** → the dose → record **administered** or **not administered**
(a note is required if not given). A dose not recorded within 60 minutes of its time alerts the
parent and admins automatically.

### Log a behaviour incident (ABC)
**ABC Tracker** → student → **Log Incident**. Fill in:
- **Antecedent** — what happened just before (tags + description);
- **Behaviour** — what the child did; **Consequence** — what followed;
- **Severity** 1–5, **location**, time;
- **Sensory factors** — e.g. loud noise, bright light, crowding — select all that apply.

Use the filters above the charts to look for patterns by date, time of day, location or sensory
factor. "Showing X of Y" tells you how many incidents the filter kept.

### Record goal progress (mastery %)
**Students** → student → **Care Plan**:
- **Current goals: record measured progress** — enter the percentage of trials the child succeeded
  in (0–100) and save.
- **Mastered goals** — choose how the child did and, if you measured it, enter **Measured %**.
  A typed percentage takes precedence over the category: 80 % or more = Achieved, 40–79 % = In
  Progress, below 40 % = Not demonstrated.

A mastered goal scoring below 80 % starts an early regression check for the therapist.

## 4. Therapist tasks

### Care plan and IEP
- **Students → Care Plan:** add or edit goals and milestones; the family is notified.
- **IEP Builder:** draft goals (AI suggestions are a starting point — review every one), check the
  conflict warnings, then **Finalise**. Finalised versions cannot be changed; create a new version
  instead. If someone else saved first you will be asked to reload.

### Regression alerts
Alerts appear on the student's page when a mastered goal declines. Review the observations, then
**resolve** with a note and, if needed, a remedial goal.

### AI behaviour analysis
**ABC Tracker** → student → **Generate Deep AI Report** (needs at least five recorded incidents).
The analysis covers all of the student's incidents, not only the current filter. **Save to Care
Plan** stores it on the care plan. If the AI service is unavailable you get factual counts only.

### Sessions
- **Teletherapy:** schedule a session (only possible when the child has an approved, linked parent);
  join at the time; write the session summary afterwards.
- **Dynamic Therapy Timeline:** schedule in-centre sessions; conflicts for you or the child are
  flagged.
- Reminders go automatically to you, the parent and linked caregivers 24 h and 1 h before.

### Home plan
**Home Plan** → assign an activity with instructions and frequency. Follow the parent's logs and
adherence, reply in the activity thread, upload demonstration videos and give feedback on practice
clips (links expire after five minutes; clips are deleted after 30 days).

## 5. Both roles

### Messages
**Messages** → student → type and press **Enter**. The whole care team, including the family, sees
it. Messages are permanent.

### Documents & Reports
Upload assessments, reports or letters (PDF, PNG, JPEG, DOCX, ≤10 MB) with a title and category.
Download with the icon; delete your own uploads. **Generate progress report** produces a printable
summary for 30, 90 or 180 days.

### Panic alert
Use the **Panic Alert** page or the floating red button. Choose the student, emergency type and
location, then confirm. All centre admins are alerted immediately (and by email when configured).
Follow the centre's emergency procedure — the app does not replace it.

## 6. Good practice
- Record observations on the day; the charts and reports are only as good as the records.
- Use the child's first name only in free-text notes where possible; never record other children's
  details in one child's record.
- Everything you view and change is audit-logged.
