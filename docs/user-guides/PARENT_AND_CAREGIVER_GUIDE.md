# Special Care 360 — Guide for Parents and Caregivers

This guide is for a child's **parent** (guardian) and for **caregivers** — relatives or home carers
the centre has linked to the child. Where the two differ, it says so.

## 1. Getting started

### Create your account
1. Open the Special Care 360 website and select **Register**.
2. Enter your name, email and a password (at least 8 characters).
3. For **Role**, choose **Parent** or **Caregiver (family / home carer)**.
4. Enter the **Center ID** the centre gave you (for example `center-001`).
5. Submit. You will see **Awaiting Approval** — this is normal.

The centre checks every new account before it can see any child's information. Once approved:
- a **parent** is linked to their child when the centre enrols the child;
- a **caregiver** is linked by the centre to the specific child (up to three caregivers per child).

### Log in
1. Select **Parent Login** or **Caregiver Login**.
2. Enter your email and password.

If you see "Incorrect email or password", check both and try again. The message is deliberately the
same for either mistake, so nobody can use the login page to find out who has an account.

### Forgot your password
Type your email on the login page and select **Forgot password**. If an account exists, a reset
link is emailed to you. Check your spam folder.

### Install it on your phone
- **Android (Chrome):** open the site → menu (⋮) → **Install app**.
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the site → **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**.
- On a computer (Chrome or Edge), click **Install** on the banner at the bottom of the page.

Care records are never stored on the phone: without a connection the app shows an offline
page, and the latest information appears once you are back online.

It then opens like an app from your home screen. Use the menu button (top left) to move between
pages.

## 2. What you can see

| Page | Parent | Caregiver |
|---|---|---|
| Students — your child's profile, medical and emergency info, care plan and progress chart | View | View |
| Daily Care — the day's meals, mood, hygiene and notes | View | View |
| ABC Tracker — behaviour incidents and patterns | View | View |
| Messages — talk to the care team | Yes | Yes |
| Documents & Reports — shared files and progress reports | Yes | Yes |
| IEP Builder, Therapy Timeline, Teletherapy, Home Plan | Yes | — |
| Fees & Billing | Yes | — |

You only ever see your own child. Nothing on these pages is example data: if a section is empty,
nothing has been recorded yet; if something fails to load, the page says so.

## 3. Everyday tasks

### Read today's daily care
**Daily Care** → choose your child. The **Daily Digest** shows meals, the mood timeline and the
teacher's notes. You are notified when the teacher submits it.

### Follow progress on goals
**Students** → your child → **Care Plan**. Each goal shows milestones and a **Goal progress**
chart. The dotted line at 80 % is mastery. Points marked as estimates were recorded as a category
rather than a measured percentage.

### Look at behaviour patterns
**ABC Tracker** → your child. Use the filters (dates, time of day, location, sensory factor) to see,
for example, whether incidents cluster in the afternoon or around noise.

### Message the care team
**Messages** → your child → type and press **Enter**. Everyone on your child's team (parent,
caregivers, teacher, therapists, centre admins) sees it and is notified. Messages cannot be edited
or deleted, so check before sending. For emergencies, phone the centre.

### Share or download documents
**Documents & Reports** → choose your child.
- **Upload:** choose a PDF, photo (PNG/JPEG) or Word file up to 10 MB, add a title and category,
  select **Upload**. The team is notified.
- **Download:** select the download icon. The link works for five minutes.
- **Delete:** you can delete documents you uploaded.

### Print a progress report
**Documents & Reports** → **Generate progress report** → choose 30 days, 90 days or 6 months →
**Print / Save as PDF**. It summarises goals, behaviour and daily care from the records the team
entered. It is not a clinical assessment.

### Reminders
You receive a notification 24 hours and 1 hour before each therapy or teletherapy session (the bell
icon at the top). Select a notification to open the related page.

### Parents only
- **Teletherapy:** at session time, open **Teletherapy** and join the call.
- **Home Plan:** see activities the therapist assigned, log each practice, reply in the activity's
  thread, and optionally upload a short practice video (you choose whether AI may summarise it).
- **Fees & Billing:** open an invoice and either choose **Pay online** (card or wallet on
  Safepay's secure page; the invoice shows **paid** as soon as you return) or pay by bank transfer
  using the details shown and enter the transaction reference; the invoice then shows **paid** once
  the centre confirms the transfer.

## 4. Privacy
- Use your own account; don't share passwords.
- Every view of a child's record and every change is logged, and centre admins can review that log.
- Ask the centre if you want a caregiver added or removed.

## 5. Getting help
Contact the centre administrator for account approval, linking a child or caregiver, billing
questions, or anything that looks wrong in your child's records.
